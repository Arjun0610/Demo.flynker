01

Want something made just for you — a fit, a fabric, a heritage weave? No mainstream marketplace connects you to the maker.

The Flynker Way

01 · AI Styling

Tell Aria the occasion, your vibe, your budget — even upload a selfie. She reads skin tone, body type and personal style to hand-pick real, shoppable outfits from the live catalogue. Not a script. A conversation.

Business Model

Six revenue streams, one platform.

Flynker pairs immediate transaction revenue with long-term, high-margin recurring revenue — spanning marketplace, creator commerce, and B2B SaaS.

01

Transaction Commission

Primary revenue. A % of GMV charged to the brand, creator, or designer on every marketplace, Style Capsule, or bespoke order.

02

Sponsored Listings

Brands pay for featured placement in the feed, category pages, and Aria's AI recommendation priority.

03

Creator Commerce Fees

Platform fee on brand–creator collaboration deals negotiated through Flynker's channels — separate from the product commission.

04

Flynker Pro (Subscription)

User subscription: unlimited Aria AI Stylist sessions, ad-free browsing, early access to drops, and free express delivery.

05

Designer Pro (B2B SaaS)

Brand/designer subscription for advanced analytics, trend prediction, demand forecasting, and priority catalogue placement.

06

Native Advertising

In-feed, AI-targeted ads on a CPM/CPC model — similar to Instagram ads, but fashion-vertical focused.
