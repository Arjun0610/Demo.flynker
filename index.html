The Problem

Great D2C brands. Broken economics.

A small fashion brand pays twice: once in ads to win the customer, and again when the parcel comes back. Most returns come down to one question nobody answered before checkout: “Will it suit me?”

CAC

Paying to be seen

Independent labels compete for the same Instagram and Google feeds, and the cost of every new customer keeps rising.

RTO

Paying to get it back

Cash-on-delivery fashion orders get refused or returned. Shipping is paid twice and stock comes back damaged.

FIT

Photos aren't reality

Skin tone, body type and fit can't be seen on a catalogue model, so the parcel disappoints at the door.

GAP

Inspiration lives elsewhere

Creators sell the look on Instagram, but buying it means screenshots, five tabs and a guess.

Solving D2C's two biggest bottlenecks

Two engines built into Flynker.
One cuts RTO. One cuts CAC.

Every D2C fashion brand in India is squeezed from both ends: it pays more and more to win a customer, then loses money again when the parcel comes back. Other marketplaces leave both problems with the brand. Flynker builds the fix into the platform.

Bottleneck 1 · RTO

The Confidence Engine

Answers “Will it suit me?” before the shopper adds to cart, not at the doorstep.

Why orders come back: wrong fit, a colour that doesn't suit, a product that looked different in the photo, or an impulse COD order the buyer never really wanted.

  1. 01
    Reads the shopperAria analyses skin tone, matched to a published reference scale, plus body type and style from one selfie.
  2. 02
    Matches fit and colourRecommendations are filtered to what flatters this person, not what's trending for everyone.
  3. 03
    Shows it before buyingVirtual try-on, Outfit Studio and AI-generated complete-look previews.
  4. 04
    Proves it on real peopleGarments worn and styled by creators in Flynks and Style Capsules, not flat catalogue shots.
  5. 05
    Removes the incentive to oversellCreators are paid only on delivered orders, so nobody pushes orders that will come back.

Result: the shopper buys with confidence, so fewer COD refusals and returns, less shipping paid twice, and less damaged stock.

Bottleneck 2 · CAC

The Creator Corner

An in-built creator sales force, paid only for delivered sales instead of ad impressions.

Why acquisition is so expensive: every brand bids for the same Instagram and Google feeds, pays up front for reach, and pays again to re-win the same customer.

  1. 01
    Creators and designers live inside the appCreator Corner is a directory of fashion creators and designers, each with a profile, Flynks, Style Capsules and a channel.
  2. 02
    They style brands' productsBrands list once; any creator can feature their pieces in looks and short videos.
  3. 03
    Every sale is attributedCapsule and product sales are tracked to the creator automatically at checkout.
  4. 04
    Paid on delivery, enforced in the databaseCommission is credited only when the order is delivered, so brands pay for real sales, not clicks.
  5. 05
    Channels bring buyers backFollowers return for drops and chats, so a repeat order costs almost nothing to win.

Result: no upfront ad spend for brands, and one shared audience instead of every brand buying each customer alone.

Together they reinforce each other. The Creator Corner brings shoppers in cheaply; the Confidence Engine makes sure what they buy stays bought. Every delivered order and every return teaches Aria more about what suits whom, so both engines get better as Flynker grows. See the flywheel ↓

Investor FAQ

The questions we get asked most.

Why won't Myntra or Meesho just copy this?

They can copy features, not the data. Our Confidence Engine learns from every selfie, try-on, purchase and return which styles and colours actually suit which skin tones and body types. Big marketplaces are built around a catalogue grid and ad-funded discovery; rebuilding around a personal stylist and a creator sales force means changing how they make money today. We start where they are weakest: small designers, ethnic and occasion wear, and COD shoppers.

How exactly do you cut returns (RTO)?

Most fashion returns come down to "it didn't suit me" or "it didn't fit". Before checkout, Flynker reads the shopper's selfie for skin tone and undertone, matches colours against a fixed reference scale, and shows the outfit styled on real creators. A shopper who is confident before paying is far less likely to refuse the parcel at the door. Measuring this reduction is the first milestone of the round.

How do you get customers without burning money on ads?

Creators are our sales force. They build shoppable Style Capsules, share them, and earn a commission only after the order is delivered, so we pay for results, not for clicks. On top of that, SEO, Google Shopping and community channels bring repeat shoppers back for free. Paid ads are a top-up, not the engine.

Why would brands and designers join a new marketplace?

Because we fix the two costs that hurt them most: ads to find customers and returns that come back. Brands get a creator sales force and fit-confident shoppers in one place, plus a simple app to manage stock and orders. In Phase 1 our target is to onboard 120+ D2C fashion brands across South India, in women's and men's wear, footwear, beauty and lifestyle, alongside independent designers.

How does Flynker make money?

A commission on every order from day one. Then sponsored listings and creator collaboration fees in year 1, shopper and designer subscriptions in year 2, and native ads in year 3. Brands hold the stock, so the model is asset-light.

Is the product real, or a mock-up?

Real. The four apps run on a live backend: place an order in the shopper app and it appears in the brand and creator apps within seconds, and commissions are credited only when the order is marked delivered. The AI stylist and skin-tone analysis run on Google's Gemini models. For demos we use sample brands and creators; they are test data, not signed partners. Our own store, flynker.com, is fully live.

Isn't the AI expensive to run?

No. Reading a selfie or styling a look with today's multimodal models costs a fraction of a rupee per request, small next to the cost of one returned parcel. As volume grows we can cache results and move frequent tasks to smaller models.

Why start with Indian ethnic wear and COD?

It has the hardest economics and the richest craft. COD return rates are high, which makes the pain obvious and our impact measurable. If personalisation works there, it works anywhere. Indian ethnic and occasion wear also has buyers worldwide, which is our path to global after India.

What will ₹1.5 Cr achieve?

18 months of runway to launch all four apps, onboard 120+ brands and the first creator cohorts, and prove with data that the flywheel lowers returns and marketing cost. Split: 40% product and AI, 30% brand and creator acquisition, 20% team, 10% operations, legal and buffer. Those milestones make Flynker seed-ready.

What are the biggest risks, and how do you handle them?

Three: creators not joining, brands not listing, and the RTO reduction being smaller than we expect. We handle them by paying creators only on delivery (no cash burned on creators who don't sell), starting with South Indian D2C brands we can meet in person, and testing every change on our own live store before scaling it. We share real numbers in monthly investor updates, good or bad.
